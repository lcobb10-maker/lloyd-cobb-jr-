# Selected engineering case studies

These summaries demonstrate professional competencies without disclosing proprietary systems. Measurements are reported experimental results, not client revenue or production guarantees.

## 1. Interactive commerce evaluation
**Problem:** Evaluate competing customer experiences before making a production investment.

**Work:** Defined acceptance criteria, compared prototype interactions, and documented failed checks and evidence quality.

**Result:** The engineering record reports 17 passing Python tests and browser-checked interactions. Real customer transactions and conversion are not established.

**Skills:** Requirements analysis, prototype evaluation, test design, stakeholder communication.

## 2. Release assurance
**Problem:** Reduce the risk of releasing a complex interactive business demonstration.

**Work:** Applied repeatable build checks, automated accessibility checks, and regression testing across viewport sizes.

**Result:** The release record reports 11 of 11 automated checks passing. Physical-device validation and customer outcomes remain open.

**Skills:** QA automation, release governance, accessibility, risk assessment.

## 3. Performance engineering
**Problem:** Improve responsiveness while retaining visual quality.

**Work:** Compared baseline and candidate under controlled local browser conditions.

**Result:** Reported median first paint changed from 592 ms to 400 ms (32.4% reduction in elapsed time), with three runs per version. This is a small lab comparison, not a production benchmark.

**Skills:** Experimental design, performance measurement, technical trade-off analysis.
