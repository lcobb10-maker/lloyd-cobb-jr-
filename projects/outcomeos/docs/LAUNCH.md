# OutcomeOS — pilot-first path to paying customers

## Day 0: qualification (evidence not yet established)
1. Offer an independent 90-minute Action-to-Proof pilot to one decision-maker in a selected industry (recommend local accelerators or service/retail operators before claiming enterprise breadth).
2. Show a running local demo; collect a problem statement and a measurable target. Do not offer real enterprise software access while authentication/billing security and deployment remain incomplete.
3. Pitch a **scoped service pilot** at a test price of $2,500, with defined preparation, workshop, follow-up and reporting deliverables. Final price depends on discovery, cost, procurement and rights.
4. If buyer accepts, prepare contract, authorization to record/use evidence, invoice via an approved merchant platform and secure data collection. No transaction/contract is created by this repo.

## 30-day delivery sequence
- Days 1–3: interview stakeholders, baseline metric, name owner, validate constraints and obtain consent.
- Days 4–7: build the pilot card, cost assumptions and assigned steps; deliver workshop.
- Days 8–21: run check-ins, resolve blockers, attach evidence in a secured approved system.
- Days 22–30: compare target/actual, report limitations and next actions, request renewal or permissioned case study.

## B2B licensing eligibility gate
- At least three paid customers; at least two independently corroborated outcomes; documented positive contribution after labor/support and CAC; explicit reuse rights to methodology; clear privacy/security controls; working production tenant access and billing; minimum two customers who ask for repeat access.
- A $5,000 annual license is a **hypothesis**, not a guaranteed best product.

## Four example industries
- HR: manager-owned 30-day onboarding project; measure completed job-relevant assignments.
- Property operations: ticket-to-resolution pilot; measure documented response time, avoiding private tenant data.
- Technology incubator: prototype-to-five-customer trial; measure written feedback and consented active use.
- Events: sponsor activation follow-up; measure permissioned introductions and sponsor-qualified interest.
