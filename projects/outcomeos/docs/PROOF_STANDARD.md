# Proof Standard & License Rules (draft specification)

## Action Proof Card
For every pilot capture: problem, affected users, pilot scope, accountable owner, date, partner commitments, funding hypothesis, primary metric, target, actual, evidence and next decision.

Status meanings: Idea = proposed; In progress = assigned activity underway; Blocked = owner recorded obstacle; Completed = owner self-reports completion. **Completed is not independently verified**. An external customer, sponsor, payment or ROI claim requires corroborating records and consent to publish.

## Customer isolation
One user owns one private organization in the reference MVP. All resource reads/updates include org scope. Future B2B: multiple organizations, users with scoped roles, invite acceptance, row-level access controls, encrypted at-rest data, audit trails, retention policies, export/delete rights and SOC 2 preparation when economically warranted.

## White-label licensing boundaries
License methodology under agreement; do not imply partner firms or the original event endorse the tool. Brand customization requires client-owned assets and permission. Define allowed seats, term, revocation, derivative works, support scope, invoicing, chargeback/refund terms and who owns outcome data.

## MPI definition
MPI is a custom proposed ratio: `(monthly revenue - monthly variable fulfillment - monthly customer support - other monthly variable expenses) * expected retained months / customer acquisition cost`.

For CAC zero or unknown, report N/A. For negative contribution, result is negative. Do not substitute published price for measured realized revenue, or exclude sales labor and onboarding. Track gross profit, cash conversion, churn, payback, and utilization separately. MPI is not a universally recognized accounting measure.
