## What can appear here

- Dated narrative field notes and the questions driving the work.
- Interface-level descriptions of features already safe to discuss.
- Small, separately authored public verification utilities.
- Sanitized measurements, explicit failed tests, and status distinctions.
- Public references that are credited and properly linked.

## What stays outside this repository

- Private application or agent source, hidden prompts, planning heuristics and unpublished ICM implementation.
- Production access, APIs, account identifiers, environment files, infrastructure addresses, security details and customer data.
- Supplier terms, partner contracts, private pricing inputs and unreleased product strategy.
- Original high-resolution artistic assets not expressly cleared for public use.
- Detailed descriptions of potentially patentable, not-yet-filed methods.

**The original sketches discussed at project inception were posted by a separate artist. They are not rehosted here and are not represented as Lloyd's original artwork.**

## Publication gate

Before committing any new dispatch, review it for intellectual property, privacy, credentials, unpublished invention disclosure, third-party copyrights and factual accuracy. If the disclosure risk is unclear, keep the detail in a private record and publish a higher-level statement instead. Never copy the private AWS project or the private Shopify/game repositories into this public portfolio.

Publishing on GitHub does not itself secure trade secrets or patent rights. Other users can view and fork public content on GitHub under its terms. A public disclosure may also affect patent filings, especially outside the United States. Do not publish enabling technical details of a potentially patentable invention before appropriate legal review.

This document explains publishing intent; it is not a guarantee that publicly revealed information cannot be copied or independently developed.
